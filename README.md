# Rebate Forms

Rebate Forms is a free web tool for HVAC contractors and their office staff that cuts down the paperwork behind heat pump rebate applications.

Rebate programs usually ask for the same details — the customer, the home, the existing heating and cooling, the new equipment — again and again across a spreadsheet application and several PDF forms. Rebate Forms asks for each detail once, in a simple step-by-step form, and fills out every form for you.

## What it fills out

Built around the PSEG Long Island residential heat pump rebate paperwork:

- **Rebate application workbook (Excel)** — customer and contractor information, existing equipment, each new heat pump system, integrated controls and electrical, and optional water heating. The workbook's own formulas then calculate equipment details, Manual J pass/fail and rebate amounts.
- **Residential Household Income Screening application (PDF)** — property, owner, utility and household income details for income-eligible customers.
- **Assignment Letter (PDF)** — when the rebate is paid to the contractor.
- **Project Completion Certification (PDF)** — once the installation is finished.

Everything downloads together in one zip, with any photos or documents attached to the job. The PDFs stay editable, and signatures are left blank so the customer can sign.

## Helpful extras

- **Equipment lookup** — search the NEEP cold-climate heat pump list by model or AHRI number, and the ENERGY STAR heat pump water heater list by brand or model.
- **Saved jobs** — jobs save automatically as you type, so a job can be started at the sale and finished after the install.
- **Company info** — enter your company details once and they are added to every application.

## Privacy

Rebate Forms runs entirely in your web browser. Customer information, saved jobs and generated forms stay on your own computer — nothing is uploaded to a server, and nothing typed into the site is stored in this repository.
